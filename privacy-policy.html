Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Static Architecture & No Server Database

This website operates as a pure static informational website. We do not operate a backend user database, do not create customer accounts, and do not track users across the web.

2. External Communication Applications (WhatsApp & Email)

When you use the enquiry features on this website:

  • WhatsApp Actions: Selecting WhatsApp opens the external WhatsApp web or mobile application with your prefilled product enquiry. No enquiry data is captured or saved on our website servers. The message is transmitted only when you click "Send" within WhatsApp.
  • Email Actions: Selecting Email opens your device's default email client (via a standard mailto: link) with a structured message draft. Transmission occurs through your own email provider.

3. Cookies and Tracking

This static website does not set analytical tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party profiling scripts.

4. B2B Commercial Data Use

Information communicated directly by trade partners (such as contact name, company name, phone, and product inquiries) is used solely by Baburam Trading or Om Sai Enterprise to respond to commercial distribution inquiries, discuss order requirements, or share verified technical specifications. We do not sell or lease trade information.
