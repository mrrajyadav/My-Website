Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

This website functions as a static corporate showcase and trade inquiry platform. We do not maintain server-side user tracking databases, cookies, or telemetry trackers. Information submitted through our direct inquiry forms (such as contact person name, company name, phone number, and commercial requirements) is transmitted strictly via client-side channels (such as WhatsApp or standard email clients) directly to our trade desks.

2. Use of Information

Any trade inquiry details provided voluntarily by commercial clients and partners are utilized solely for responding to distribution requests, providing price quotations, verifying technical specifications (TDS/SDS), and managing B2B customer relationships.

3. Confidentiality of Commercial Data

Neither Baburam Trading nor Om Sai Enterprise sells, rents, or leases trade inquiries to third parties. All distributor communications and supplier details remain confidential.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For questions regarding this privacy statement or our commercial handling practices, please contact our administrative desk through the official contact points listed on this portal.
