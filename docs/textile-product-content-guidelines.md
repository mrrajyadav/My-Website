## 2. Technical Documentation Standard
Every product listed within the Textile Chemicals catalog must maintain the following documentation standards:
1. **Manufacturer TDS (Technical Data Sheet)**:
   - Contains chemical character, appearance, ionic nature, pH (1% aqueous solution), solubility, compatibility, and recommended dilution techniques.
   - Sourced directly from verified domestic or multinational chemical producers.
2. **Manufacturer SDS / MSDS (Safety Data Sheet)**:
   - Full 16-section standard document outlining GHS hazard classifications, personal protective equipment (PPE) mandates, spill handling, storage thresholds, and first aid procedures.
3. **Batch COA (Certificate of Analysis)**:
   - Provided upon commercial consignment dispatch, verifying active solid content, viscosity, specific gravity, and pH consistency against batch specifications.

## 3. Commercial Packaging Standards
Chemical orders are fulfilled in standardized heavy-duty industrial shipping containers:
- **Carboys**: 50 kg HM-HDPE carboys with tamper-evident caps (ideal for specialty enzymes, optical brighteners, and concentrated silicone emulsions).
- **Drums**: 200 kg high-density polyethylene (HDPE) barrels or epoxy-coated steel drums (standard for scouring aids, levellers, binders, and softeners).
- **IBC Totes**: 1,000 kg Intermediate Bulk Containers (IBC) with discharge valves (utilized for bulk commodities, sizing polymers, and continuous pad ranges).
- **Dry Sacks**: 25 kg or 50 kg multi-layer woven polypropylene bags with inner LDPE liners (for flakes, starches, salts, and powder formulations).

## 4. Technical Content Structure for Product Profiles
Each individual static HTML product page adheres strictly to the 19-section dossier format:
1. **Header & Navigation**: Brand lockup, full site navigation, IndiaMART trade CTA.
2. **Breadcrumbs**: Hierarchical navigation chain (`Home` > `Chemicals` > `Textile Chemicals` > `Category` > `Product`).
3. **Hero & Summary**: Product name, category kicker, short description, primary action buttons (IndiaMART + TDS Request).
4. **Quick Specifications Table**: Code, class, function, stage, compatible fibres, applications, machinery, and standard packaging.
5. **Wet Processing Role & Technical Overview**: Contextual function in industrial processing lines.
6. **When Is It Used?**: Exact sequence timing (e.g. exhaust pretreatment, pad-dry-cure, wash-off, stenter).
7. **Why Is It Used?**: Quality problems eliminated (e.g. resist spots, uneven strike rates, harsh touch, poor crock fastness).
8. **How It Works (Mechanism)**: Chemical mode of action (surfactant micellization, charge neutralization, film cross-linking, chelate ring formation).
9. **Technical Selection Factors**: Guidelines for mill purchase officers and dye masters.
10. **Compatibility & Co-Auxiliaries**: Ionic harmony, electrolyte tolerance, and bath synergy.
11. **Process Limitations & Operational Precautions**: Overdosing hazards, pH boundary limits, and shearing constraints.
12. **Storage, Packaging & Handling Protocol**: Temperature limits, shelf life, and ventilation requirements.
13. **Technical Documentation & Trader Disclosure**: Mandatory trader disclaimer box.
14. **Complementary / Related Products**: Links to 3 to 4 related products in the same category.
15. **Commercial Sourcing Call to Action**: Direct trade enquiry on IndiaMART and Surat desk phone/email.
16. **Footer**: Division details, legal policies, and corporate portal navigation.
