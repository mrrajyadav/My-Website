DYES-COLORANTS • Coloration Family (Distinct from Auxiliaries)

Textile Dyes and Colourants

Chromophoric colourant molecules that chemically bond or physically disperse within textile fibres. Treated as a distinct product family separate from processing auxiliaries.

Key Chemical Families

  • • Reactive Dyes (Cold & Warm Brand, Bifunctional, High Exhaustion)
  • • Disperse Dyes (Low, Medium & High Energy)
  • • Acid & Metal-Complex Dyes
  • • Direct Dyes for Cellulosics
  • • Vat Dyes & Solubilised Vat Systems
  • • Sulphur Dyes
  • • Pigment Colour Dispersions

Key Processing Applications

  • ✓ Cellulosic fibre coloration
  • ✓ Polyester, acetate, and triacetate coloration
  • ✓ Polyamide (Nylon) and wool coloration
  • ✓ Garment piece dyeing and printing pastes
Buyer Sourcing & Evaluation Notes: Evaluated by light fastness, wash fastness (ISO 105-C06), rubbing fastness, perspiration fastness, exhaustion rate, fixation percentage, and shade consistency from lot to lot.
