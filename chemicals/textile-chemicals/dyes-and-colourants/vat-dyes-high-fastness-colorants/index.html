1. Wet Processing Role & Technical Overview

Vat Dyes (High Fastness Colorants) is a specialized dye colourant belonging to the Vat Dyes classification within Textile Dyes and Colourants. It is utilized primarily for coloration applications across textile wet processing operations.

Functional Purpose: Engineered to deliver high-performance coloration across Cotton, Linen, Military Fabrics substrates in industrial textile manufacturing.

2. When Is It Used? (Processing Sequence)

Applied via alkaline reduction (hydrosulfite + caustic soda) followed by air or chemical re-oxidation.

3. Why Is It Used? (Operational & Quality Challenges Solved)

The industry standard for institutional hospital linens, military camouflage, and workwear exposed to repeated harsh industrial laundering.

4. How Does It Work? (Chemical Mechanism & Mode of Action)

Reduced water-soluble leuco anions penetrate fibres, where subsequent oxidation regenerates the original insoluble pigment locked inside.

5. Technical Selection Factors for Mill Buyers

Substrate affinity and exhaust build-up performance across light, medium, and heavy depths. Light fastness, wash fastness (ISO 105-C06), and perspiration fastness specifications. Fixation temperature window, electrolyte/alkali requirement, and wash-off efficiency. Tone reproducibility and minimization of shade metamerism under multiple light sources. Compliance with international eco-standards (free of banned carcinogenic aromatic amines)

6. Chemical Compatibility & Co-Auxiliary Synergy

Compatible with recommended class-specific dyeing auxiliaries, salts, and alkali donors. Do not blend incompatible dye classes (e.g. cationic basic dyes with anionic reactive dyes). Filter dissolved dyestuff through fine mesh sieve before charging machine additions

7. Process Limitations & Operational Precautions

Shade reproducibility depends heavily on substrate preparation consistency and water hardness. Inadequate washing-off leaves hydrolysed surface dye resulting in poor wet rub fastness

8. Storage, Packaging & Handling Protocol

Store powder and liquid dyes in airtight containers in a cool, dry storeroom away from steam and moisture. Prevent dust cloud generation during weighing.
