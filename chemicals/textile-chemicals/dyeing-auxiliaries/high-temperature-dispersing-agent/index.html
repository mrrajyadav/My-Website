1. Wet Processing Role & Technical Overview

High-Temperature Dispersing Agent is a specialized chemical auxiliary belonging to the Dispersing Agents classification within Textile Dyeing Auxiliaries. It is utilized primarily for dispersing applications across textile wet processing operations.

Functional Purpose: Engineered to deliver high-performance dispersing across Polyester, Triacetate, Polyester Blends substrates in industrial textile manufacturing.

2. When Is It Used? (Processing Sequence)

Dosed into disperse dye preparation tanks and dyeing machines before heating up to 130°C.

3. Why Is It Used? (Operational & Quality Challenges Solved)

Disperse dyes tend to agglomerate at high temperatures, creating dye specks, filtration marks on yarn packages, and poor rubbing fastness.

4. How Does It Work? (Chemical Mechanism & Mode of Action)

Adsorbs onto hydrophobic disperse dye crystals, imparting a high negative zeta potential that provides strong electrostatic repulsion.

5. Technical Selection Factors for Mill Buyers

Substrate fibre compatibility and blend sensitivity,Operating bath pH tolerance and thermal stability window,Foaming behavior under high-shear machine circulation,Compatibility with adjacent bath auxiliaries and dyestuffs,Wastewater biodegradability and compliance with regional ETP limits

6. Chemical Compatibility & Co-Auxiliary Synergy

Verify ionic compatibility with co-applied surfactants before bath mixing,Maintain bath pH within recommended operational range specified in manufacturer TDS,Conduct lab dilution trial when combining with high electrolyte concentrations

7. Process Limitations & Operational Precautions

Do not mix directly in concentrated form with oppositely charged surfactants,Exact processing parameters must be optimized based on mill water hardness and liquor ratio

8. Storage, Packaging & Handling Protocol

Store in original sealed containers in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight and freezing temperatures. Standard shelf life 12 months under recommended storage.

Standard Industrial Packing: 50 kg Carbouys, 200 kg HDPE Drums, 1,000 kg IBC Totes
