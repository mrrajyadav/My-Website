OBA-WHITENING • Whiteness Enhancement

Optical Brighteners and Whitening Agents

Fluorescent whitening agents (FWAs) that absorb invisible ultraviolet radiation and re-emit it in the visible blue-violet spectrum, counteracting natural yellowness to yield brilliant white finishes.

Browse Products (7) Enquire on IndiaMART

Key Chemical Families

  • • Stilbene-Based Cellulosic Optical Brighteners
  • • Disulphonated & Tetrasulphonated Cotton OBAs
  • • Coumarin & Benzoxazole Polyester OBAs
  • • Polyamide (Nylon) & Wool Optical Brighteners
  • • High-Temperature Stable OBAs for Synthetic Blends
  • • Optical Brightener Boosters & Shading Tints

Key Processing Applications

  • ✓ Exhaust application on bleached cotton knits and yarns
  • ✓ Padding and stenter thermofixation on polyester and synthetic blends
  • ✓ Combined peroxide bleaching and brightening baths
  • ✓ Medical, hospitality, and shirting textiles requiring high CIE whiteness index
Buyer Sourcing & Evaluation Notes: Evaluated by Berger and Ganz whiteness index, greening/yellowing threshold concentration (over-brightening limit), light fastness, and compatibility with peroxide or resin baths.
