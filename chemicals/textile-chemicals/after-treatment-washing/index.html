After-treatment and Washing Chemicals

Chemicals designed to remove unfixed surface dyes, neutralise residual processing alkali/acid, prevent re-deposition during rinsing, and chemically fix dyes to maximise wet and wash fastness.

Key Processing Applications

  • ✓ Post-reactive dyeing soaping sequences
  • ✓ Reduction clearing of dyed polyester at 70-80°C
  • ✓ Direct dye fixation for apparel fabrics
  • ✓ Garment wash cycles to prevent pocket/label staining
Buyer Sourcing & Evaluation Notes: Evaluated by wash fastness improvement (ISO 105-C06), reduction in wash-off bath cycles to conserve water and steam, non-impairment of original shade tone, and absence of formaldehyde emissions.
