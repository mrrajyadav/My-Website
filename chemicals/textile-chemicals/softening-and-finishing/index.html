  • • Nonionic Fatty Acid Softeners

  • • Hydrophilic Modified Polyether Silicone Fluids

Key Processing Applications

  • ✓ Stenter pad-dry-cure finishing of wovens and knits
  • ✓ Exhaust softening in winches and soft-flow machines
  • ✓ Toweling and knitwear requiring instant water absorbency
  • ✓ Garment laundry tumble finishing
Buyer Sourcing & Evaluation Notes: Evaluated by surface smoothness (hand-feel), shade alteration/yellowing index upon stenter drying, rewettability (water droplet absorption time), tear strength preservation, and shear stability in padding mangles.
