1. Wet Processing Role & Technical Overview

Textile Spinning Lubricant is a specialized chemical auxiliary belonging to the Spinning Lubricants classification within Fibre, Yarn, Spinning and Sizing Chemicals. It is utilized primarily for lubricating applications across textile wet processing operations.

Functional Purpose: Engineered to deliver high-performance lubricating across Cotton, Wool, Viscose, Blended Fibres substrates in industrial textile manufacturing.

2. When Is It Used? (Processing Sequence)

Sprayed onto raw cotton or synthetic staple bales during blow-room opening or carding preparation.

3. Why Is It Used? (Operational & Quality Challenges Solved)

Minimizes roller lapping, suppresses airborne micro-dust and fly, and improves drafting regularity for superior yarn count consistency.

4. How Does It Work? (Chemical Mechanism & Mode of Action)

Coats individual staple fibres with a balanced cohesive oily film, optimising inter-fibre friction during high-speed draft attenuation.

5. Technical Selection Factors for Mill Buyers

Substrate fibre compatibility and blend sensitivity. Operating bath pH tolerance and thermal stability window. Foaming behavior under high-shear machine circulation. Compatibility with adjacent bath auxiliaries and dyestuffs. Wastewater biodegradability and compliance with regional ETP limits

6. Chemical Compatibility & Co-Auxiliary Synergy

Verify ionic compatibility with co-applied surfactants before bath mixing. Maintain bath pH within recommended operational range specified in manufacturer TDS. Conduct lab dilution trial when combining with high electrolyte concentrations

7. Process Limitations & Operational Precautions

Do not mix directly in concentrated form with oppositely charged surfactants. Exact processing parameters must be optimized based on mill water hardness and liquor ratio

8. Storage, Packaging & Handling Protocol

Store in original sealed containers in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight and freezing temperatures. Standard shelf-life parameters and storage guidelines are specified in the manufacturer Technical Data Sheet.

Standard Trade Packaging: 50 kg Carbouys, 200 kg HDPE Drums, 1,000 kg IBC Totes (Subject to manufacturer dispatch format).
