1. Wet Processing Role & Technical Overview

Mercerising Auxiliary is a specialized chemical auxiliary belonging to the Mercerising Aids classification within Textile Pretreatment Chemicals. It is utilized primarily for wetting applications across textile wet processing operations.

Functional Purpose: Engineered to deliver high-performance wetting across Cotton substrates in industrial textile manufacturing.

2. When Is It Used? (Processing Sequence)

Added directly into the concentrated caustic soda impregnation bath on mercerising ranges.

3. Why Is It Used? (Operational & Quality Challenges Solved)

Concentrated caustic soda has high surface tension; without mercerising wetting agents, caustic cannot penetrate grey cotton yarn bundles.

4. How Does It Work? (Chemical Mechanism & Mode of Action)

Remains fully soluble and surface-active in 25%–30% NaOH solutions, accelerating fibre swelling, lustre, and tensile development.

5. Technical Selection Factors for Mill Buyers

Draves wetting speed and penetrating kinetics at operating bath temperatures. Chemical stability in concentrated alkaline caustic soda scouring liquors. Emulsification capacity for natural cotton waxes, pectins, and synthetic knitting oils. Low foaming profile under high-turbulence jet and winch machine liquor circulation. Surfactant biodegradability and compliance with regional wastewater discharge limits

6. Chemical Compatibility & Co-Auxiliary Synergy

Verify ionic compatibility (anionic vs nonionic) when pairing with enzymatic desizing agents. Stable across neutral to strongly alkaline liquor ranges (pH 6.0 to 12.5). Compatible with simultaneous scouring and hydrogen peroxide bleaching formulations

7. Process Limitations & Operational Precautions

Overdosage in high-speed jet machines may induce foaming requiring defoamer addition. Ensure thorough post-scour hot water rinsing to prevent alkali carryover into dyeing baths

8. Storage, Packaging & Handling Protocol

Store in original drums in a cool, covered chemical depot away from direct frost and extreme heat. Reseal drum bungs tightly after sampling.
