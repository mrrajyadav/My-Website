1. Wet Processing Role & Technical Overview

Catalase Enzyme Peroxide Killer is a specialized chemical auxiliary belonging to the Peroxide Killers classification within Textile Bleaching Chemicals. It is utilized primarily for bleaching neutralisation applications across textile wet processing operations.

Functional Purpose: Engineered to deliver high-performance bleaching neutralisation across Cotton, Cellulosics, Blended Fibres substrates in industrial textile manufacturing.

2. When Is It Used? (Processing Sequence)

Added to the rinse liquor following peroxide bleaching prior to reactive dyestuff introduction.

3. Why Is It Used? (Operational & Quality Challenges Solved)

Residual peroxide oxidatively destroys reactive dye chromophores, leading to pale shades, unlevelness, and lot rejection.

4. How Does It Work? (Chemical Mechanism & Mode of Action)

Catalase enzyme active sites rapidly cleave hydrogen peroxide molecules into pure water and inert dissolved oxygen gas without chemical byproducts.

5. Technical Selection Factors for Mill Buyers

Hydrogen peroxide stabilization efficiency under alkaline steam or exhaust bleaching. Silicate-free sequestering capability to prevent insoluble scale on fabric and machinery. Chelation capacity for polyvalent catalytic heavy metal ions (Fe, Cu, Mn). Target CIE whiteness index attainment with minimal cellulose fiber depolymerisation. Controlled oxygen liberation kinetics matching continuous or exhaust dwell times

6. Chemical Compatibility & Co-Auxiliary Synergy

Compatible with standard caustic soda, hydrogen peroxide, and nonionic scouring wetters. Do not mix directly in undiluted concentrated state with strong mineral acids. Stable in high-pH alkaline bleaching liquors (pH 10.5 to 11.5)

7. Process Limitations & Operational Precautions

Inadequate stabilization can trigger catalytic oxycellulose localized pinhole holes. Excessive stabilizer concentration may retard bleaching kinetics and reduce whiteness

8. Storage, Packaging & Handling Protocol

Store in cool, well-ventilated chemical storage away from direct sunlight, reducing agents, strong acids, and combustible materials. Keep containers tightly closed.
