BLEACHING • Bleaching & Whiteness Preparation

Textile Bleaching Chemicals

Formulations used in oxidative and reductive bleaching to destroy natural coloring matter in fibres and achieve uniform base whiteness before dyeing or optical brightening.

Key Chemical Families

  • • Hydrogen Peroxide (Industrial Concentration)
  • • Organic Non-Silicate Peroxide Stabilisers
  • • Sodium Silicate Stabilising Systems
  • • Bleaching Wetting Agents
  • • Peroxide Killers (Catalase Enzymes)
  • • Reducing Bleaching Systems (Sodium Hydrosulphite alternatives)

Key Processing Applications

  • ✓ Cotton yarn and knit bleaching
  • ✓ Continuous bleaching ranges for woven fabrics
  • ✓ Peroxide neutralisation baths prior to reactive dyeing
  • ✓ Full-white finish fabric production
Buyer Sourcing & Evaluation Notes: Evaluated by peroxide decomposition rate control, preservation of fibre tensile strength, absence of silicate scale build-up in machinery, and complete elimination of residual peroxide to prevent dye reduction.
